FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Do I need an accountant for my limited company?

Although most limited companies are not required to appoint an accountant, professional support can help with accounts, tax returns, bookkeeping and reporting deadlines.

Can you handle both company and personal tax returns?

Yes, our services cover corporation tax and Self Assessment for eligible clients, including directors, landlords and sole traders.

What is an outsourced accounts department?

It is an external team that manages agreed parts of your finance function, such as bookkeeping, invoicing, reconciliations, payroll coordination and reporting.

Do you provide statutory audit services?

We can discuss audit readiness and assurance support. Statutory audits can only be provided where the relevant audit registration and authorisation are in place; please contact us to confirm availability.

Can I move from my current accountant?

Yes. We can discuss the transition process and, where appropriate, request professional clearance and relevant records.

How are fees calculated?

Fees depend on your business, transaction volume and services required. We can provide a tailored quotation after discussing your needs.
